# Hello, World! Welcome to My Blog

It's alive! 🎉

I've been meaning to start a blog for a while now, and here we are. This is my tiny corner of the internet where I'll be sharing:

- **Things I'm learning** — bite-sized notes and tutorials
- **Projects** — what I'm building and why
- **Thoughts** — random musings on tech, life, and everything in between

## Why a blog?

I'm a firm believer in *learning in public*. Writing things down forces me to actually understand them. And if someone else stumbles onto one of these posts and finds it useful — even better.

## The tech stack

This blog is as simple as it gets:

- Plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript
- Blog posts are Markdown files rendered client-side
- Hosted on GitHub Pages — zero cost, zero fuss

No frameworks. No build tools. Just files and a browser.

> "Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication." — Leonardo da Vinci

## What's next?

I'll be posting whenever I have something worth sharing. No schedule, no pressure. Just writing for the fun of it.

Thanks for stopping by! ✏️
